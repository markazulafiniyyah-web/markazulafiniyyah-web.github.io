Program overview

Learn with personal guidance.

Our online Tajweed classes focus on practical recitation. Students study Makharij al-Huruf, essential Sifat and applied Tajweed rules while reading directly to a teacher who identifies and corrects mistakes.

What you will develop

  • Correct Makharij al-Huruf and pronunciation
  • Practical understanding of essential Sifat
  • Applied rules including Madd, Ghunnah, Idgham and Ikhfa
  • More fluent and confident Qur'an recitation
