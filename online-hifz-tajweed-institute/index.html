A connected learning path

Memorize the Quran without leaving correct recitation behind.

Students searching for an online Hifz and Tajweed institute often need two forms of guidance at the same time: a reliable memorization routine and a teacher who listens carefully to every recited passage. Our one-to-one format brings both together. New memorization is supported by revision, while pronunciation and practical Tajweed are corrected during the lesson.

01

Structured online Hifz

The Hifz path uses a personal plan suited to the student's current level and pace. Sabaq develops new memorization, Sabqi reinforces recently learned passages, and Manzil protects older memorization through continued revision.

  • Personal memorization targets
  • Sabaq, Sabqi and Manzil routine
  • Direct correction during recitation
  • Progress at a sustainable pace

Frequently asked questions

Online Hifz and Tajweed classes

Can Hifz and Tajweed be studied together online?

Yes. Recitation is corrected as memorization develops, helping students avoid repeating pronunciation mistakes while learning new passages.

Are these classes suitable for children and adults?

Yes. The amount of new memorization, revision and Tajweed practice can be adjusted to the learner's age, level and available study time.

What happens in an online Hifz lesson?

A lesson can include new memorization, recent revision and older revision through the Sabaq, Sabqi and Manzil method, together with direct recitation correction.

Can a beginner start with Tajweed before Hifz?

Yes. Learners who need stronger reading foundations can begin with Nazra, pronunciation and practical Tajweed before starting a full memorization plan.
