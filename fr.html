Our Programs

From your first letters to confident recitation — one journey, three essential programs.

Hifz-ul-Qur'an

A structured memorization journey guided by experienced Huffaz — daily Sabaq, Sabqi and Manzil cycles, personalized plans, and rigorous revision until the entire Qur'an rests firmly in your heart.

  • Personalized Hifz plan
  • Daily Sabaq · Sabqi · Manzil
  • Ijazah upon completion
Learn more

Tajweed

Master Makharij al-Huruf, Sifat and the rules of recitation — from Noorani Qaida foundations to fluent, beautiful Tilawah — exactly as the Qur'an was revealed.

  • Noorani Qaida fundamentals
  • Makharij & Sifat mastery
  • Fluent Nazira recitation
Learn more

Nazra Qur'an

Build fluent, accurate Qur'an reading from the foundations upward — letter recognition, joining, vowel marks and guided daily recitation with close teacher correction.

  • Noorani Qaida foundations
  • Fluent Qur'an reading
  • Daily guided recitation

FAQ

Les réponses à vos questions.

How do the online classes work?

Classes are one-to-one video sessions of 30–45 minutes via Zoom, Skype or WhatsApp — your choice of platform, teacher and schedule.

What ages can enroll?

We welcome students from age five with no upper limit. Curricula are adapted for children, teens and adults.

Are female teachers available for sisters and children?

Yes. Qualified female teachers teach sisters and young children upon request.

How much does it cost?

Affordable monthly plans start after three free trial classes and a free assessment. Family discounts are available.

Can I earn an ijazah?

Yes. Students who complete the prescribed standards in Hifz receive ijazah with sanad from qualified scholars.

Which languages are classes taught in?

Instruction is available in Arabic, English and Urdu, with support in other major languages.
